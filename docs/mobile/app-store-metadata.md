## App Name

Nexus: AI Workspace & Meetings

## Subtitle (iOS) / Short Description (Android)

Meetings, Chat, Docs, and Tasks powered by AI.

## Full Description

Welcome to Nexus, the all-in-one Global Work Operating System. We have collapsed your video meetings, team chat, document editing, and project management into a single, lightning-fast application.

**FEATURES:**

- **HD Video Meetings**: Host crystal clear WebRTC calls with screen sharing, background blur, and noise suppression.
- **Smart Chat**: Channels and DMs with offline support. Read and reply even on the subway.
- **AI Copilot**: Schedule meetings or summarize missed conversations using your voice. "Nexus, summarize the marketing channel."
- **Tasks & Sprints**: Kanban boards and calendar views that sync instantly across your team.

_Requires a Nexus Workspace account._
