# MOBILE PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** June 2026

## 1. Information Collection
When using the Nexus Mobile App, we collect:
- **Device Information**: Push Notification Tokens (APNs/FCM) to deliver realtime messages.
- **Microphone & Camera**: Required for hosting or joining WebRTC video meetings. We do NOT record audio/video unless explicitly initiated by the host.
- **Speech Recognition**: Voice commands sent to the AI Copilot are securely transmitted to our AI Gateway.

## 2. Offline Data Storage
To provide an offline-first experience, Nexus stores encrypted copies of your chats, documents, and tasks locally on your device using SQLite/Isar. This data is cleared if you log out.

## 3. Analytics
We use anonymized crash reporting to improve app stability. You may opt out in Settings.
